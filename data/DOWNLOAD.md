# Data acquisition

Raw third-party datasets are not distributed in this repository. Obtain the records below, retain the upstream attribution, process them with the supplied preparation entry points, and verify the analysis-ready files with `checksums.tsv`.

- AUS-Wheat 2015: https://doi.org/10.25909/5becfa45c176f
- Ward wheat: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7700012.v1
- GABI genotype: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.n02v6wwzc
- GABI phenotype: https://doi.org/10.5447/ipk/2022/18

The checksums identify the exact analysis-ready files used by the manuscript; an upstream archive may require documented preprocessing before its checksum matches.
